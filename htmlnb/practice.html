About Uttrakhand

Uttarakhand is a beautiful northern state of India, often called “Devbhoomi” (Land of the Gods) because of its many temples and pilgrimage sites.

Uttrakhand is known for its beauty, spirituality and biodiversity. The people in uttrakhand are trustworthy and helpful.

Famous Places in Uttrakhand


1. Nainital: A picturesque hill station known for its beautiful lakes and scenic views.

2. Rishikesh: A spiritual town famous for yoga and adventure sports like white-water rafting.

3. Haridwar: A sacred city where the Ganges River exits the Himalayas, known for its religious significance and the Kumbh Mela festival.

4. Jim Corbett National Park: A renowned wildlife sanctuary and the oldest national park in India, home to Bengal tigers and diverse flora and fauna.

5. Auli: A popular skiing destination with stunning views of the Himalayas.

Unique Food of Uttrakhand


1. Aloo Ke Gutke: simple yet delicious dish made with boiled potatoes, mustard seeds, and spices.

2. Bhatt Ki Churdkani: A traditional dish made from black soybeans cooked with spices and yogurt.

3. Kafuli: A nutritious dish made from green leafy vegetables like spinach and fenugreek, cooked with spices and yogurt.

4. Bal Mithai: A popular sweet made from roasted khoya (milk solids) coated with sugar balls.

5.Singodi: A traditional sweet made from rice flour, jaggery, and coconut, wrapped in a leaf and steamed.
